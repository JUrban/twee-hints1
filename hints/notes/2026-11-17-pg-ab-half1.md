# Parental guidance at scale: half1 from zero, 2 hours, sum filter 0.25 against the defaults

**Question.** Does the sum filter's +23 −0 on the 200-goal testbed (2026-11-17-pg-sum-filter.md) hold on a
whole benchmark half run as the system runs, from zero?

**Setup.**
* abs/pg_ab.sh 0.25, started 17 Nov 16:54. Two arms of abs/or_test.sh, 6 workers each, 2 hours:
  * PGB, the control: solve_new.sh's new defaults (twee-e44, cascade 5,20,80,320, `--no-fresh-states`,
    `--mix 0.1 --fail-order`, live transfer every 300 s).
  * PGA: the same through /project/bin/twee-pg, which is twee-e48 with `--pg-threshold 0.25`. With the
    flags off, e48 gives output identical to e44.
* Goals: sets/half1, 64 of the 129 originals (random, seed 17), from zero. The context is loops/EMPTY, so
  no lemmas or hints come from outside.
* Noise: two identical 2-hour half1 runs differed by 185 proofs, about 1% (ORA/ORD, REPORT 2au).
* Outputs: loops/PGA, loops/PGB, PGA.log, PGB.log. Count with abs/setcurve.py, which counts only the
  set's goals.

**Results** (goals of half1 proved, measured at 18:38 and at the end, 18:55, with `abs/setcurve.py sets/half1 PGA PGB --at ...`:
proof-file times against each arm's config.json):

* Goals only one arm proved: PGA 828, PGB 251 (the set's goals). Proofs feed later goals as lemmas and hints,
  so these counts compound and are not independent wins.
* By cascade level, as runs (r1/results.tsv): PGA 16,537 of 21,257 at 5 s, 72 of 273 at 20 s, 0 of 2 deeper;
  PGB 15,940 of 20,293, 98 of 293, 3 of 12.
* Where the one-arm goals were proved: PGA-only 665 at 5 s and 40 at 20 s; PGB-only 177 and 32 (1 at 80 s).
  The rest came from the transfer worker.

* REPORT 2av's "at 10 minutes PGA 9,458, PGB 8,986" was written at 17:02, so it was read before minute 10.
  It needs correcting.
* Proof files that are rewritten get a later time, so earlier checkpoints can read slightly lower than they
  did at the time.

For comparison, ORH (half1 from zero, 2 h, with `--no-fresh-states`; REPORT 2au) had 18,476 at 2 hours; its
settings are close to PGB's but I have not checked that they are identical.

**Reading.**
* +577 at 2 hours is three times the noise of identical runs (185). The filter wins.
* The gain is front-loaded: +1,406 at 10 minutes, then shrinking until 100 minutes, then flat (+596, +587,
  +577). The filter makes the easy proofs faster and so reaches the hard tail sooner. It does not open the
  tail much more: in the last hour PGA gained 458, PGB 802.
* The 20 s level is not clearly hurt. The share of 20 s runs that succeed is lower in PGA (26% against
  33%), but those are different, harder goals, since PGA had already proved more. Goals only one arm proved
  at 20 s: 40 against 32. A direct test would run PGB's 98 20-second proofs with the filter.
* The testbed's +23 −0 of 200 at 5 s was not representative of the scale gain (+3% at 2 hours). It was
  chosen among failures, as expected.
* Depcheck on PGA: no circular or out-of-order lemma use (2026-11-17-lemma-circularity.md).

**Decision.**
* PG at 0.25 is solve_new.sh's default from 19:00 (TWEE twee-e48, PG=--pg-threshold 0.25; PG= turns it off).
  Production already runs it (abs/ul41.sh, twee-e50 at 0.25, since 17:25).
* Next: Z5 (the whole benchmark from zero with PG), the 20 s test above, and threshold tuning at scale.
  0.25 was set on 5 s runs; the long levels might want a lower one.

# The heads of the long gaps, with chain hints (exp12)

Started 18 Nov 2026, 11:21 UTC. Directory /project/hintwork/exp12; scripts and sets in hints/exp/exp12_*.

## Why

exp11 (notes/2026-11-18-wall-breaking.md) aimed at each original's first unsolved goal, but most of those are
isolated holes. In the gap structure at 11:21, the 96 gaps of 20 or more consecutive unsolved goals hold 4,045 of
the 6,370 unsolved. 83 of them reach their original's top goal.

A gap's first goal (its head) has its proven predecessor right below it. Its proof gives the next goal a proven
neighbour, a new context for production's 5 s retries. That is how one merged deep proof opened nil3_27 462-477
(REPORT 2ax). Chain hints beat the pool at 80 s and 320 s (exp8, exp3).

## Set and setting

* The heads of all 96 gaps of length >= 20 at 11:21 (heads.tsv: head, gap length, reaches the top; goals96). This
  is the whole set, not a sample.
* Commands captured in production's context, as in exp9 and exp11 (`LOOP_PRINT_CMD=1 LOOP_KEEP_TMP=1 SCHED=
  XEVERY=100000 CASCADE=5 timeout 1500 bash exp3/solve_cap.sh goals96 loops/UL cap/CAP 3 0.1`). The hints are
  then replaced by the chain (exp11/mkchain.py: sibchain.py, chain_records.tsv, UL's 10 nearest proven siblings
  below).
* Stage A: 80 abstract s, 4 workers, starting when exp11's stage 1 ends. Stage B: the unproved heads at 320 s.
* Proofs are merged into UL every 10 minutes. For each merged head I count the goals of its gap that production
  (or the transfer) then proves.
* No pool control: production's pool runs in round 5 failed on every head. The deepest level reached was 320 s
  for 45 heads, 80 s for 49 and 5 s for 2 (checked 11:23).

## Launch (11:22)

`bash stage.sh 2423550 2420988` (exp/exp12_stage.sh). It waits for the capture and exp11's stage-1 runner, merges
exp11's last proofs, then tbify (80 / 320 s), `mkchain.py`, `run.py A.cmds A 4`, and `run.py B.cmds B 4` on the
rest. merges.log records every merge.

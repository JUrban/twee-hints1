# One 20 s run for every unsolved goal that never had more than 5 s (20 Nov)

Written 20 Nov 2026, 15:32 UTC, at launch. Script exp/sweep20.py; goals exp/sweep20_goals.txt.

## Why

* In the non-joined steps test ([notes/2026-11-20-step-join.md](2026-11-20-step-join.md)) both arms proved the same
  4 of 100 unsolved goals: production's transfer options at 20 s with today's lemma context, no hints. The steps
  added nothing.
* Production had run those 4 goals 20-80 times each, every run at 5 s (the 5 s level L0 and the 5 s variants).
* **2,895 of production's 3,606 unsolved goals never had a production run above 5 s** (2,491 of them unit goals).
  The ratio order gives the CPU to the 5 s members with the best yields, and the deeper levels go to few goals.
* Production's own loop proved 2 goals in its last 2,000 runs (4.9 h of run time). A 4% yield at 20 s would be about
  5 proofs per CPU hour, ten times that.

## Setting

* The 2,895 goals, random order (seed 1), in chunks of 300.
* Before each chunk, exp/trust.py over production. The chunk's lemma context is production's trusted solved list
  at that moment, so new proofs (the sweep's own too) become lemmas for later chunks.
* Each chunk is one `transfer.py run` with exp/stepjoin.py's options:
  * production's transfer options: rm4t with the theory block, 6 sibling and 30 kNN lemmas with subsets,
    twee-pg61, abstract time 2000;
  * 20 s, no hints;
  * `--keep-out`, so every proof has its lemma record;
  * proofs into loops/UL; the proofs themselves kept (exp/proofq.py).
* 2 workers, beside Z6.

      python3 exp/sweep20.py loops/SW20 exp/sweep20_goals.txt -j 2

## Measures

* Proofs per chunk and per CPU hour (loops/SW20/sweep.tsv), against production's members' yields (portfolio rule:
  a member that proves goals nothing else does is kept whatever its rate).
* Unit against conditional goals; whether the yield falls along the chunks.
* If it holds at several per CPU hour, production gets a 20 s fresh member for goals without a run above 5 s.

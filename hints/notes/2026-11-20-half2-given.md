# The derived-rule transfer from zero on half2 (exp41)

Written 20 Nov 2026, 09:16 UTC, at launch. Script abs/exp41.sh ([hints/exp/exp41.sh](../exp/exp41.sh)).
Follows exp40 ([notes/2026-11-20-from-zero-given.md](2026-11-20-from-zero-given.md)).

## Why

* exp40 on half1: PGE 19,591 at 2 hours against PGD's 19,309 (+282). Identical runs differ by about 185. exp29's
  +392 on half1 did not replicate on half2 (exp30, −118), so one half is not enough.
* solve_new.sh runs the transfer by default since 09:13 (GIVEN=1). This tests that default on the other half.

## Set and setting

* **Set:** sets/half2, the 65 originals not in half1, 25,847 goals. From zero: context loops/EMPTY.
* **P2E (the arm):** abs/or_test2.sh with SET=sets/half2, so solve_new.sh's defaults: P2D's setting plus GIVEN=1
  (loop.py `--given-scan loops/P2E_GF`; `givenmatch.py live loops/P2E_GML --loops loops --dst P2E --dirs
  P2E,P2E.xfer --gfail P2E_GF --set sets/half2 --every 300 -j 2 --cap 60 --no-inst` with production's transfer
  options). 6 workers, 2 hours, the transfer every 300 s, `SCHED=`.
* **Control:** exp30's P2D (19 Nov 12:45-14:46): the same solve_new.sh without the GIVEN lines (diff checked at
  09:15), 19,383 at 2 hours. No new control, to save 8 CPUs. The machine's load differs; loop.py has changed since
  only by options that are off here.
* **CPUs:** 6 workers, the transfer's 2 and the live loop's 2 at times. Production goes to 4 workers (ul74 J=4).

## Measures

* `abs/setcurve.py sets/half2 P2D P2E --at 10,30,60,90,120`; one-arm goals; the live loop's runs and CPU by kind.
* If P2E ≥ P2D + 185, the gain replicates and GIVEN=1 stays. If within noise, pooled with half1. If it loses by more
  than the noise, GIVEN goes back to 0.

## Results (11:20)

| arm | 10 min | 30 min | 60 min | 90 min | 120 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P2A (exp30) | 11,489 | 17,345 | 19,041 | 19,442 | 19,501 |
| P2D (exp30, control) | 11,369 | 17,397 | 18,963 | 19,323 | 19,383 |
| **P2E (GIVEN=1)** | 11,190 | 17,182 | 19,198 | 19,977 | **20,152** |

* **P2E against P2D: +941 / −172, net +769.** The union is 20,325. Against P2A: +826 / −176. That is far beyond
  the noise of about 185, so the half1 gain (exp40, +282) replicates on half2, and more strongly. **GIVEN=1 stays
  the default.**
* **CPU:** the live loop took 3.05 CPU h over the 2 hours, at most 2 workers. At P2D's rate between 90 and 120 min
  (60 proofs in 30 minutes on 8 CPUs, about 15 per CPU h), 3 more CPU hours would give P2D about 45 proofs.
* **The live loop:** 752 runs, 501 proofs in 3.05 CPU h, 352 of them goals P2D never proved.

  | kind | runs | proofs | CPU h | proofs per CPU h |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | the rule's ancestry, 20 s | 383 | 339 | 0.63 | 541 |
  | the log's prefix, 40 s | 42 | 19 | 0.63 | 30 |
  | same statement in a stronger theory, 20 s | 327 | 143 | 1.79 | 80 |

* **The cap starved the best kind.** Each cycle ran 60 hit goals, the cap shared with the same-statement runs, and
  145–192 hits were still pending at the end. The same-statement runs took 59% of the loop's CPU at a seventh of
  the ancestry runs' yield.

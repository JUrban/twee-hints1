# Step joins: unsolved goals closed by rewriting with proof steps of weaker theories (20 Nov)

Written 20 Nov 2026, 10:43 UTC.

## Question

Does an unsolved unit goal's ground (skolemized) equation rewrite to one normal form with the union of the proof
steps of proven unit goals in theories within the goal's? If so, those steps as lemma axioms give twee a proof in
about a second. Production never puts them together: its lemmas are proven goals, not steps of other proofs.

## Method

* **exp/joincheck.py:**
  * ordered ground rewriting (size, then root name, then arguments), each step in both directions where the
    variables allow;
  * the bank per theory is the hints files (proof steps) of every proven unit goal whose original's axioms are
    within the goal's, plus the theory's own equational axioms;
  * `--trusted` restricts the bank to trusted proofs (exp/trust.py, [p9 negation bug](2026-11-20-p9-negation-bug.md)).
* **exp/stepjoin.py:**
  * for each join, the steps used become a sketch (as hints) and a `.ax` file with the source goal per step;
  * transfer.py gives them to twee as lemma axioms `skax_<i>_<source goal>`, with production's options, `--keep-out`
    and 20 s;
  * USEDAXIOMS records the sources, and trust.py resolves them.
* **Set:** all unsolved unit goals of production at 10:39 (3,170; the conditional goals are left out). Bank: trust1
  of loops/REVER2 (47,570 trusted). Two shards, `--steps 3000`.

## Commands

    for i in 0 1; do python3 exp/joincheck.py loops/JOIN/all_$i.tsv -n 100000 --trusted loops/REVER2/trust1.trusted --shard $i/2; done
    python3 exp/stepjoin.py loops/STEPJ loops/JOIN/all_0.tsv loops/JOIN/all_1.tsv --trusted loops/REVER2/trust1.trusted -j 2

## Results

* **10:42, 1,881 of 3,170 checked:** 9 joins, and all 9 proved, in 0.3–6.4 s each (12 s wall on 2 workers).
* For example, nil3_23_32_to_26__goal_507 (`rd(e,k(ld(X0,e),X1)) = k(X1,rd(e,X0))`) used three steps of Ka1_eq_Ka2
  proofs: `ld(k(X0,X1),e) = rd(e,k(X0,X1))`, `k(X0,X1) = ld(k(X1,X0),e)` and `k(X0,ld(X1,e)) = k(X0,rd(e,X1))`.
* **10:46, all 3,170 unit goals:** 21 joins (9 + 12 in the two shards), and stepjoin proved **21 of 21** (40 s wall
  on 2 workers).
* Cost: the two shards took about 6 minutes each, about 0.2 CPU h with the proofs. That is **about 100 proofs per
  CPU hour**, and every proof is of a goal production had failed. (One pass over a backlog: the next passes only
  find what new proofs add to the banks.)
* **The conditional goals (10:45):** joincheck `--cond` takes the one hypothesis `s = t` as a ground rule,
  normalised by the bank and oriented. The rule is sound for that goal only, and is cleared after it.
  * Control, 60 proven conditional goals without their own steps: 33 joined (unit control: 41 of 60).
  * The 514 unsolved conditional goals (10:50): 2 joins (aK2_eq_aK3_d__goal_587, nil3_1K_e__goal_485), both proved.
* **In all: 23 joins of 3,684 unsolved goals, 23 proofs.**
* **Live member from 10:50:** abs/stepjlive.sh (exp/stepjlive.sh) runs both checks and stepjoin every 30 minutes,
  the first at 11:20, with the newest trust file as the bank while the re-verification runs.

## Cross-run joining with the goal's own failed run (pilot, 11:32)

* **Question:** the steps alone join 23 of 3,684 unsolved goals. Do they join more together with the rules the goal's
  own failed run derived? Those rules are consequences of that run's axioms and lemmas.
* **Pieces:**
  * loop.py `--given-keep DIR`: each failed unit-goal run's given rules go to DIR/<goal>;
  * joincheck `--own-given DIR`: a goal's own rules join the bank for that goal only;
  * if this pays, stepjoin will take the own rules as hints, not axioms, so the proof's record still lists only
    named lemma axioms.
* **Pilot:** exp/owng_pilot.py, 30 unsolved unit goals (seed 1), each run once at 60 s with production's transfer
  options and the trusted context (loops/REVER3/trust1.trusted), 2 workers. Then joincheck with the trusted bank
  plus the own rules. Output in loops/OWNG.

      python3 exp/owng_pilot.py loops/OWNG -n 30 --time 60 -j 2 --trusted loops/REVER3/trust1.trusted

* **Result (12:07): 0 of 30 joined.**
  * The 30 runs proved nothing (1,997 s wall on 2 workers). They kept 9k-25k given rules each.
  * 18 of the 30 goals normalised with no step at all. 12 used 1-5 steps, and only in 3 of them was one an own
    rule.
  * The sides of an unsolved goal are mostly irreducible under both banks: the goal's own run's rules are oriented
    by twee's order, which joincheck's size order often reverses, and they did not join the goal in twee either.
  * At the steps' own rate (23 of 3,684, 0.6%) 30 goals would give 0.2 joins, so this cannot show a small gain.
    It shows no large one. Closed: not worth 2 CPU minutes a goal. `--given-keep` and `--own-given` stay in the
    code, off.

## A bigger bank: the from-zero arms' proofs of the same goals (12:40)

* joincheck `--banks LOOP,...` also takes proof steps from other loops' hints files, for the goals production
  proved (and trusts). stepjoin reads them from there, and still names each step after its goal.
* Only loops started from the empty context: Z0–Z4, P2A/D/E/F, PGA–PGE, ORA–ORH (22). None of them ran Prover9.
  UA, UB, ABa–ABe and SMT are out: they started from production's state on 15 Nov, partly after the first unsound
  proof.
* Their proofs come from other contexts, so they have other steps. The bank for aK2_eq_aK3_d grows from 42,520
  equations to 60,757.
* Test: unit shard 0/2 (1,562 goals) against the same shard with production's bank alone (cycle 1 of stepjlive2:
  0 joins).

      python3 exp/joincheck.py loops/JOIN/banks/u0.tsv -n 100000 --trusted loops/REVER4/trust1.trusted --shard 0/2 \
        --banks Z0,Z1,Z2,Z3,Z4,P2A,P2D,P2E,P2F,PGA,PGB,PGC,PGD,PGE,ORA,ORB,ORC,ORD,ORE,ORF,ORG,ORH

* **First run (12:40-12:51): 12 "joins", 11 of them through one false line.** Z4's hints for lra2aa__goal_214
  hold `X0 = t(X0,X1)` (line 21), a printed goal line: loop.py writes twee's HINTS-GOAL lines into the hints file
  together with the lemmas, unmarked. stepjoin's model check refused all 11. The twelfth (aK2eq_1234__goal_372, 1
  step) is in neither run's result below, so it used a line the filter now drops.
* **joincheck now drops such lines (12:52).** A bank line whose sides have different variables must hold in the
  theory's Mace4 models (verdicts cached in loops/JOIN/modelcache/<theory>). Same-variable lines stay unchecked
  here: the audit found no false one in production, and stepjoin checks every step it uses.
* **Second run (12:53-13:31): 0 joined of 1,557**, with 2,264 false lines left out over the banks. Production's bank
  alone also joined 0 of this shard (stepjlive2's first cycle).
* **Verdict:** the arms' proofs add 43% more equations but no joins. They are proofs of the same goals, so their
  steps rewrite to the same normal forms. Closed; `--banks` stays as an option.

## Non-joined goals: are their normalising steps useful lemmas? (13:33)

* **Question:** for an unsolved goal that does not join, the bank's steps still rewrite its sides part of the way.
  Do they help twee as lemma axioms (and hints)?
* **Set:** 100 of the 592 goals of the bank shard above (loops/JOIN/banks/u0m.tsv, shard 0/2 with the arms'
  banks, false lines dropped) whose sides some step rewrote; random with seed 1 (exp/stepn_goals.txt). All are
  unsolved after production's many attempts. No step is false in a model.
* **Arms, at the same time, 1 worker each, 20 s:**
  * STEPN: the steps as lemma axioms `skax_<i>_<goal>` and as hints, plus production's options;
  * STEPN0: the same with `--no-steps` (no steps, no hints).
  Proofs go to production (`--live-dst UL`) with their records.

      python3 exp/stepjoin.py loops/STEPN loops/JOIN/banks/u0m.tsv --trusted loops/REVER4/trust2.trusted -j 1 \
        --time 20 --nonjoined 100 --seed 1 [--no-steps]

# Step joins: unsolved goals closed by rewriting with proof steps of weaker theories (20 Nov)

Written 20 Nov 2026, 10:43 UTC.

## Question

Does an unsolved unit goal's ground (skolemized) equation rewrite to one normal form with the union of the proof
steps of proven unit goals in theories within the goal's? If so, those steps as lemma axioms give twee a proof in
about a second. Production never puts them together: its lemmas are proven goals, not steps of other proofs.

## Method

* **exp/joincheck.py:**
  * ordered ground rewriting (size, then root name, then arguments), each step in both directions where the
    variables allow;
  * the bank per theory is the hints files (proof steps) of every proven unit goal whose original's axioms are
    within the goal's, plus the theory's own equational axioms;
  * `--trusted` restricts the bank to trusted proofs (exp/trust.py, [p9 negation bug](2026-11-20-p9-negation-bug.md)).
* **exp/stepjoin.py:**
  * for each join, the steps used become a sketch (as hints) and a `.ax` file with the source goal per step;
  * transfer.py gives them to twee as lemma axioms `skax_<i>_<source goal>`, with production's options, `--keep-out`
    and 20 s;
  * USEDAXIOMS records the sources, and trust.py resolves them.
* **Set:** all unsolved unit goals of production at 10:39 (3,170; the conditional goals are left out). Bank: trust1
  of loops/REVER2 (47,570 trusted). Two shards, `--steps 3000`.

## Commands

    for i in 0 1; do python3 exp/joincheck.py loops/JOIN/all_$i.tsv -n 100000 --trusted loops/REVER2/trust1.trusted --shard $i/2; done
    python3 exp/stepjoin.py loops/STEPJ loops/JOIN/all_0.tsv loops/JOIN/all_1.tsv --trusted loops/REVER2/trust1.trusted -j 2

## Results

* **10:42, 1,881 of 3,170 checked:** 9 joins, and all 9 proved, in 0.3–6.4 s each (12 s wall on 2 workers).
* For example, nil3_23_32_to_26__goal_507 (`rd(e,k(ld(X0,e),X1)) = k(X1,rd(e,X0))`) used three steps of Ka1_eq_Ka2
  proofs: `ld(k(X0,X1),e) = rd(e,k(X0,X1))`, `k(X0,X1) = ld(k(X1,X0),e)` and `k(X0,ld(X1,e)) = k(X0,rd(e,X1))`.
* Cost: joincheck takes about 0.2 s per goal, so a full pass is about 5 CPU minutes.

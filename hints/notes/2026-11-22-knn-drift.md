# exp55: retry the goals whose kNN lemmas have drifted (`--knn-drift M`) (22 Nov 09:33)

**Question.** exp54's control proves about a sixth of its first runs on goals Z7 never proved, in Z7's final context.
Z7 had stopped trying them hours before its end. Does a retry trigger on the number of new kNN lemmas since a goal's
last fresh run, and a retry class ordered by that number, prove more from zero?

## Why (exp/knndrift.py LAA Z7, 09:33, exp54 at 38 minutes)

LAA (notes/2026-11-22-late-ab.md) runs the 3,817 half2 goals Z7 left unsolved, with Z7's 43,769 proofs as its
context. Of its first 825 first runs, 128 proved.

* Z7 had run the 128 a median of 2 times, its last run at minute 121 / 193 / 266 (quartiles) of 480; the 697 that
  failed again, at 238 / 278 / 392. 108 of the 128 were heads in Z7's final state (the goal right after a proven
  sibling): Z7's retry triggers (sibling context, a 1.25x pool, a new proof in the top 10 kNN lemmas with
  `--knn-ctx 10`) had not fired for them in Z7's last 3-6 hours, or their retries had not come up.
* Their kNN lemma sets (30, `--lemma-knn`) had changed more since Z7's last run (knn_at.tsv in Z7 and LAA): a median
  of 5 new lemmas for the proved, 2 for the failed. With 5 or more new: 74 of 185 proved (40%); with fewer: 54 of 640
  (8%). With 8 or more: 45 of 88 (51%).
* In Z8 (from zero) the `--knn-ctx` trigger already had 930-1,155 goals waiting at a time from hour 3 on, while the
  retry class proved 6 per run-hour at 6 hours. So more triggers alone would wait in that queue; the order matters.

## Change

loop.py `--knn-drift M` (default 0, off; needs `--knn-ctx`, as LATE has):
* At each goal's fresh run: the similarity of its `--lemma-knn`-th kNN lemma (kmin) is recorded, and its count reset.
* At each kNN rebuild (the `--knn-ctx` pass; every --knn-refresh seconds or 300 new proofs): for every goal waiting
  or not, the new proofs scoring above its kmin are counted (about how many of its kNN lemmas are new).
* M or more is a new context (like `--knn-ctx`'s top-10 entry), and the retry class (q) is sorted by the count,
  most first (capped at 10), then its usual order (fewest failed fresh runs, the original's rate, near before far).
* Results rows of fresh runs carry `kd=N` (the count at dispatch).

## Correction: what P3C tests (22 Nov 20:40)

`--knn-drift` does two things: the count admits goals to the retry class, and the retry class is sorted by the count.
`--knn-drift-after` (P3C) delays only the first. In P2Z's log the count alone admitted few goals: 13 by minute 30, 54
by minute 60, 179 in 6 hours (the --knn-ctx passes' "by the count only", placed in time by the passes' new-proof sums
against P2Z's solved counts at 30 and 60 minutes). So the −216 at 30 minutes is not from those admissions. It is more
likely the sort, which from the start puts high-count retries before --fail-order's usual order, or noise.

P3C therefore differs from P2Z only by 54 deferred admissions: **in effect a replica of the drift arm**, which the
+204 needs, and not a test of the early cost. It keeps running as that.

loop.py `--knn-drift-order-after MIN` (new, 20:39) keeps the usual order until MIN minutes. With
`--knn-drift-after MIN` as well, the whole drift starts at MIN. That pair is for a later test, against the control.
Smoke KDC (20:40:54-20:49:31; sets/smoke487, 2 workers, 0.12 h, solve_new.sh with `--knn-drift 5` now in LATE,
`--knn-drift-order-after 2 --knn-refresh 120`): 399 of 487 (KDB 400), no traceback, config knn_drift 5, order-after 2.

(21:08) The early cost itself is within noise: exp60's P3B, identical to its control P3A until minute 120, trailed it
by 313-381 in the first 30 minutes (notes/2026-11-22-floor-ratio-until.md, "Early readings"). P3C trailed by 222

# The parents' stored rule-model scores ordering the queue (exp35)

Written 19 Nov 2026, 17:10 UTC. Its arms start when exp33's arms end, at about 18:45, on the same 2 workers.

## Why

The user (17:00): "the forest can be as small as the weight function in the extreme, so there is a spectrum of
speed/precision tradeoffs". The record on the model's hooks in twee:
* **Pruning the generation of pairs (parental guidance, 2av).** The cheapest point, the untrained sum of the parents'
  stored rm4t scores, pays: +577 on half1 at 2 hours. Every per-pair forest tried there lost online at the same
  abstract budget:
  * the additive forest, 36-38 of 200;
  * ENIGMA forests as in the paper, 36-39;
  * against the sum's 52.
* **Ordering the queue (2ao).** Only the most expensive point was tried: rm4t, 200 trees, with the pair and both
  parents featurised again for every pair (the parents' features never change). It ran on 6 goals and cost 10× the
  wall time. The cheap end of this hook was never tried.
* twee-e62 `--pg-order F` is that cheap end. A new pair's queue score is multiplied by F ** sigmoid(q1 + q2). q1 and
  q2 are the stored raw rm4t scores of its two parent rules, the same quantity PG thresholds at 0.25. The cost is
  two lookups per pair. At F = 1 (the default) the output is byte-identical to e61 (3 goals checked).
* The scale, for comparison: pool hints multiply a matching pair's score by 0.01, and the deferral by 2.

## Set and setting

* abs/mabpo.sh = abs/mabpg.sh (exp33) with twee-pg62 and `--pg-order F` added to TWEE_PG_ARGS. Everything else is
  the same: 400 ultest goals, snapshot ULsT, fresh 20 s, PG 0.25, rm4t, 6 sibling and 10 kNN lemmas.
* **Control:** exp33's rm4t arm (loops/MP_rm4t_20, e61). Its settings are identical, and e62 at F = 1 is e61.
* **Arms:** F = 0.5 (pairs with confident parents up to 2× earlier) and F = 0.1 (up to 10×). One worker each.

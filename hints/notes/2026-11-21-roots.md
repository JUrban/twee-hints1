# Roots: which of production's proofs Z6 was one proof away from (21 Nov 00:44)

**Question.** Z6 (from zero, 8 hours, 44,095) ends 4,113 goals short of production (notes/2026-11-20-z6.md). Is
the gap scheduling (Z6 had the context for many of production's proofs and never made the right run), or depth
(production's proofs rest on lemmas Z6 never proved, chains built over days)? Mine, while exp44/45 run.

## Method

* exp/trust.py `--dump-rec loops/ROOTS/rec.tsv` (new option, off by default): each production goal's lemma record,
  the lemma goals named by the newest kept output that made production's proof (USEDAXIOMS; climbers' used.tsv).
  NOREC: no such output; UNRES: a lemma name that does not resolve to a goal.
* exp/roots.py `loops/ROOTS/rec.tsv Z6 --out loops/ROOTS/roots_z6.tsv`: for the goals production proved and Z6
  did not, a **root** is a goal whose recorded lemmas Z6 all proved (it had the context); the **reach** is the
  fixpoint of goals whose lemmas are in Z6 or reached; a root's descendants are the reached goals with it among
  their ancestors.

## Result (00:44)

* Production 48,212, Z6 44,095; production only 4,117: 3,465 with a record, 551 NOREC, 101 UNRES.
* **Roots: 190** (L1 104, L0 46, L2 26, unknown 7, variants 4, side loops 3). The reach from them is 363.
* Not reached, 3,102: 2,981 through a chain (of unreached lemmas) to a gap goal without a record, 121 only through
  cycles. Cycles exist because a record is the newest output: a re-proved goal's newest proof uses later lemmas.
* The 652 gap goals without a record: first proved by XFL 244 (the transfer loop's transfer.tsv, not read here),
  L0 103, L1 68, GML 38, ..., unknown 150; most before production's round r3 (kept outputs came later).
  Even with all of them given to Z6, the reach is 1,124 of 3,465.
* Roots by descendants: 136 have none; 6 roots are ancestors of half of the 173 reached non-roots. The biggest:
  nslm_to_aa1 1002 (L1, 36 s, 33 descendants), nil3_23_32_to_26 455 (L0, 7 s, 20), aK2_eq_aK3_1243b 446 (L1, 62 s,
  12), com_Ralt 249 (L1, 11 s, 11).

**Reading.** On the records we have, the gap is mostly depth. Z6 had the context for 190 of production's proofs,
which would lead on to about 360 goals; the rest rest on lemmas Z6 lacks. The records understate how short the
chains could be (newest outputs, missing records), so 190 to 1,124 one-step goals bound it. That the roots are
mostly L1 proofs (104 of 190) fits Z6's late phase, where L1 ran 16 times, all in its first hour: that is
exp45's question (notes/2026-11-20-yield-discount.md).

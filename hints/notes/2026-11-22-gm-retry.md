# Retrying the given-rule hits the live loop gave up on (exp56)

Written 22 Nov 2026, 11:07 UTC.

## Why

* Production's log (loops/UL75.log) repeats `given-scan: ... derived N unsolved goal(s): ['ns_to_aa1_b__goal_673']`:
  failed runs on other goals keep deriving that goal's equation as a rule (loop.py `--given-scan`, exp38,
  [notes/2026-11-20-given-transfer.md](2026-11-20-given-transfer.md)). loops/GFAIL/hits.tsv has 229 hit rows for it,
  from 32 source runs, the newest at 10:15 today.
* givenmatch.py live (loops/GML) runs a hit goal once, with the 20 s ancestry pass and then the 40 s prefix pass, and
  then lists it in loops/GML/tried.tsv for good. goal_673 was tried on 20 Nov at 04:57 (cycle 6) and failed.
* Of the 273 tried goals, 30 are still unsolved in production, and all 30 have hit rows (354 in all).
* The live loop sorts a goal's hits by ancestry size. For failed-run hits that field is always 0, so the sort falls
  back to the log path, and it took the oldest logs (in 'GFAIL/<stamp>' string order).

## Change and run

* New script, exp/gmretry.py. For every tried goal still unsolved in production, it takes the newest 3 hits of
  distinct source runs (by the millisecond stamp in the log directory's name). It then runs the live loop's two
  passes with the live loop's own code:
  * givenmatch.sketch;
  * transfer.py run, with 20 s on the ancestry, then 40 s on the prefix plus the source run's axioms;
  * GML's arguments, 1 worker.
* Proofs go into production (`--live-dst loops/UL`), as GML's do. The soundness conditions are the same as GML's:
  * the source goal's axioms are within the target's (checked by loop.py's given-scan);
  * the source runs are production's own runs on unconditional unit goals.
* Started 11:05:27 (PID 2023224), loops/GMR, log loops/GMR.log.

## Measures

* Goals proved of the 30, CPU, and which passes proved them.
* If some prove: give the live loop a retry rule (a tried goal comes back when it has a newer hit, at most once every
  few hours) and fix the hit order to newest first.

## A likely cause of the failures (22 Nov 11:21)

* In loop.py `given_scan`, the label −1 (an axiom row in the hit's rule log) comes from `givenlib.axioms(name)`,
  read without the run's problem path. That is the original's problem file, so only the original's axioms get −1.
  (`gen_save` passes the path.)
* The source run's lemma axioms are its 6 sibling lemmas and 30 kNN lemmas: proven goals, sound as axioms. They get
  label 0, so givenmatch.sketch puts them among the hints and never in the `.ax` file. A target run then has to
  re-derive proven lemmas from hints.
* In the source log of goal_673's newest hit (nslm_to_aa1 974), 1,167 of the 15,240 label-0 rules have no parents.
  The log records only critical-pair parents, so rewritten rules are parentless too. Parentlessness therefore does
  not identify the lemma axioms.
* Why passing all of them is not a fix: trust.py trusts a proof only if every lemma it used resolves to a trusted
  proven goal. A `.ax` lemma of transfer.py (srclem_) does not resolve. So the source's lemmas would have to be
  matched to proven goals (by key) and passed under resolvable names.

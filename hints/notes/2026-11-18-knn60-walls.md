# 60 kNN lemmas at 80 s on the walls (exp17)

Prepared 18 Nov 2026, 13:50 UTC; it starts when exp16's stage (PID 2433494) ends. Directory /project/hintwork/exp17;
scripts and data in hints/exp/exp17_*.

## Why

* Today's deep proofs lean on the kNN lemmas, proven goals of other originals given as axioms.
  * exp11's 15 wall proofs use on average 15.1 of their 30 kNN lemmas.
  * 13 of the 15 use one of rank 25 or more.
  * exp12's gap-head proof (aK2_eq_aK3_b 421) uses 22 of 30, and 5 sibling lemmas.
* So the cut at 30 may be binding at deep budgets.
* At 20 s, 60 kNN lemmas proved 9 against 30's 6 (+4 −1) on 150 unsolved goals (REPORT 2am). That was not
  significant, and production kept 30, at all levels.

## Set and setting

* exp11's 107 walls at 80 s (CPU cap 800 s, twee-e48), with **exp11's own chain hints** (exp11/h_chain), so only
  the problem files differ.
* Captures at 13:37-13:49 from UL (`solve_cap.sh`, as in exp11, with KNN=60 and KNN=30; EXCLUDE=goals), made
  into 80 s commands by exp6/tbify.py (K60_pool.cmds, K30_pool.cmds). exp17/mk.py swaps in exp11's hints and
  checks that all other arguments match exp11's.
* **Drift.** The context grew between exp11's capture (10:38) and these (13:37). Of the 107 problems with 30
  kNN lemmas, **94 are byte-identical to exp11's**, so exp11's runs are an exact control for them (twee is
  deterministic in abstract time; exp14 checks this on 7 walls). The other 13 run again at 30 as the control
  (K30.cmds).
* **Arm K60:** all 107 walls with 60 kNN lemmas (about 66 lemma axioms against 36; drift.tsv).
* 4 workers; first K30 (13 runs), then K60 (107).

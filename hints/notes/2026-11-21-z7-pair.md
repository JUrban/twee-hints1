# Z7 pair: today's late-phase default against the 20 Nov system, on all goals (21 Nov 12:48)

**Question.** On half2 from zero, P2O's late-phase options (exp44/45 and exp48) gave +241 to +800 over the 20 Nov
system in side-by-side arms. Do they help the same way on the whole benchmark, where Z6 (the 20 Nov system, 12
workers, 8 hours) reached 44,095?

## Why a pair, not one arm against Z6

Cross-time comparisons are biased by the machine's load. The abstract-work correction (exp/abscurve.py) removes only a
half to three quarters of it: P2R, a replica of P2O under a heavier load, still trailed P2O by 167-426 at equal work
(notes/2026-11-21-late-combo.md). So Z7 runs beside its own control instead of being compared with Z6 alone.

## Setup

* abs/z7pair.sh (exp/z7pair.sh), started 12:48:
  * **Z7:** solve_new.sh's defaults, with LATE = `--retry-class --yield-discount 2000 --reexplore 12:0.25 --knn-ctx
    10 --head-class`.
  * **Z7C:** the same with `LATE=`, the 20 Nov system that Z6 ran.
* Both: sets/all (51,784 goals), the empty context loops/EMPTY, 6 workers, 8 hours, SCHED=, transfer every 5 minutes,
  `--knn-refresh 600`, as Z6 but with half its workers. Each arm has its transfer loop and derived-rule loop (up to
  2 workers each).
* Both have today's dispatch changes (goal files at first use, the conjecture cache, the knn_at.tsv flush), the twee
  binary twee-pg61 and rule model rm4t.
* The proof worker (exp/proofq.py -j 2) runs beside them; the 20 s sweep stays paused.
* CPU: about 2 × 10 + 2.

## Measures

* `abs/setcurve.py sets/all Z7 Z7C --at 30,60,120,240,360,480`; one-arm goals (+, −, union).
* Class h and q runs, proofs and CPU hours (the mix lines, exp/quickstat.py).
* Each arm against Z6 at equal abstract work (abscurve.py, with the caveat above).
* (14:30) Portfolio measures: + and − goals, the union, and the union per worker-hour against a single arm with as many
  worker-hours (Z6, 12 workers, at the same hour). Goals new to the whole collection (all runs and production).
* Decision: identical half2 runs differ by about 185; with twice the goals, expect about 260. Z7 ≥ Z7C + 260 at 8
  hours confirms LATE on the whole benchmark. Below that, LATE stays the default (it was decided on half2), and the
  note says the whole-benchmark gain was not shown.

file), as merge.py writes them, so a merge.py loop in both directions would do it. An idea, not a planned test.

# A late-phase A/B: the goals Z7 left, with Z7's proofs as the context (22 Nov 03:00)

**Question.** Methods for the deep and head classes (exp51's `--head-time 20` first) act after the first pass, but the
3-hour half2 A/Bs from zero hardly reach them. Can an A/B that starts where an 8-hour from-zero run ended measure
them in 3 hours? And does exp51's `--head-time 20` prove more there?

## Why

* In exp53's control P2V (half2 from zero, 4 workers, 3 hours, the current defaults) the 40 s chain level ran 31
  times in 3 hours (0.55 of about 12 CPU hours): with `--floor-ratio 20` the head class waits until the first runs'
  yield falls, which on half2 is near the end of a 3-hour run. A head-class change has almost nothing to act on there.
* Z7 (all goals, 6 workers) and Z6 (12 workers) ended 8 hours at 43,767 and 44,092: twice the CPU gave 325 more.
  After 4 hours the from-zero curve is limited by hard goals, not by CPU (notes/2026-11-21-z7-pair.md, run kinds).
* Production has 4,491 of the 8,015 goals Z7 did not prove: they are provable, with more depth or better hints.

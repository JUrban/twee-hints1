# The near-oracle rule model through parental guidance (exp33)

Started 19 Nov 2026, 16:44 UTC (user, 16:40: "yes, run the oracle-through-PG test").

## Question

Does a better rule model pay through parental guidance (PG), the hook where rm4t's scores do pay (+577 on half1 at
2 hours, 2av)?
* The near-oracle rmO was tested only through the defer-once filter, on 15 Nov, before PG existed (2ad, 2af):
  * 273 of 400 against rm4t's 271;
  * work on shared proofs 1.03.
* Through PG, every learned parental model was better offline and lost online to the untrained sum of rm4t's scores
  (36-39 against 52 of 200). But none of them was an oracle, and they replaced the sum rather than feeding it
  better scores.
* If rmO's scores through the same sum filter prove clearly more, model quality matters through PG. Then better
  models (rm7t, a GNN, ENIGMAWatch-style features) are worth building. If not, the question is closed for both
  hooks.

## Set and setting

* abs/mabpg.sh = abs/mab.sh (2ad's paired test) with today's binary and PG in both arms.
* **Goals:** sets/ultest, the 400 newest round-5 proofs of 15 Nov (hard goals, proved by production's deep levels).
* **Context:** snapshot loops/ULsT, production at 15 Nov 08:10 minus these 400. `--init` copies the hints of the
  43,084 goals in its solved list only, so no test goal's own proof is in a pool. Sibling and kNN lemmas come from
  that list too. No `--online`: an arm's proofs do not change its context.
* **Runs:** fresh, 20 s at abstract time 2000 (`--max-cpu 200`), rule model with `--model-theory`, deferral threshold
  0.1 and penalty 2, 6 sibling and 10 kNN lemmas, the original's pool.
* **Binary:** twee-pg61 with `--pg-threshold 0.25 --ground-joining-branch-limit 100` (production's).
* **Arms:** M=rm4t (production's model) and M=rmO (trained on these 400 goals' own logs: AUC 0.956; at 0.1 it keeps
  99.3% of proof rules and defers 57.3% of the others). One worker each, both at once.
* PG's sum filter uses each model's own raw scores, so the two arms freeze different shares of the pairs at 0.25.
  The production threshold is the question asked here. A calibration-matched threshold for rmO is a follow-up if
  this comes out close.

## Measures

* Proved of 400, one-arm goals (+/−), work (abstract time) on shared proofs.
* Decision:
  * rmO at least 10 above rm4t (2ad's noise was about ±8 one-arm goals) means model quality pays through PG;
  * within that, the question is closed.

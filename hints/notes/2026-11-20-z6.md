# Z6: today's from-zero system on the whole benchmark, 8 hours (20 Nov)

Written 20 Nov 2026, 15:30 UTC, at launch. Script abs/z6_test.sh ([hints/exp/z6_test.sh](../exp/z6_test.sh)).

## Question

How far does today's system get from zero on all 51,784 public goals, and how does its curve continue after 2
hours? The from-zero tests since 17 Nov ran on halves for 2 hours. A half run cannot use the other half's kNN
lemmas, and most of what production has beyond a half run came through them
([notes/2026-11-20-half-gap.md](2026-11-20-half-gap.md)). The 19 Nov assessment listed a 6-8 hour from-zero run as
missing.

## Set and setting

* **Set:** sets/all (51,784 goals), empty context (loops/EMPTY).
* **System:** solve_new.sh's defaults of 20 Nov:
  * twee-pg61 (PG 0.25, ground-joining branch limit 100);
  * the cascade 5, 40, 80, 160, 320 s with chain hints from level 1;
  * the explored 5 s / 80 s portfolio (DEEP);
  * GIVEN=1, the derived-rule transfer, with solve_new.sh's default GIVENARGS (`--every 300 -j 2 --cap 60 --no-inst`): exp42 and exp43 changed nothing.
  * SCHED= and the transfer every 5 minutes, as Z4.
  * **One change from the defaults:** `--knn-refresh 600`, production's setting. By default the kNN index is rebuilt
    every 1,800 s or after 300 new proofs. Once proofs come slowly, that delays each kNN cascade step by up to 30
    minutes. In Z4's third hour the loop proved about 50 goals per half hour.
* **Workers:** 12, as Z1 and Z4; 8 hours of new cascade runs.
* **Rule model:** rm4t, trained on public proofs (so "from zero" is not fully clean, REPORT 2aq).
* **Comparison:** Z4 (abs/z4_test.sh, the 17 Nov system, 12 workers): 15,398 at 10 minutes, 35,511 at 1 hour,
  37,713 at 2 hours, 37,962 at 3 hours, 38,044 at 4 hours. Z1: 36,552 at 2 hours. The two half arms of today's system
  at 2 hours sum to 39,743 (PGE 19,591 + P2E 20,152, 2 × 6 workers).
* **Proofs kept** (the user, 15:03): every proved run is rerun with the proof printed by exp/proofq.py, into
  loops/Z6/proofs, loops/Z6.xfer/proofs and the live loop's dirs. The reruns run outside the search workers, so the
  curve is comparable with Z4's.
* **Other CPU meanwhile:**
  * production's own loop is stopped (2 proofs in its last 2,000 runs); its transfer and live loops keep running;
  * a 20 s sweep of production's unsolved goals on 2 workers (notes/2026-11-20-sweep20.md);
  * the re-proofs (REVER4) to their end; the proof worker (2); step joins paused.

## Measures

* `abs/setcurve.py sets/all Z4 Z6 --at 10,30,60,120,180,240,360,480`.
* Busy workers and runs per hour by window; the live loop's proofs by kind; where the hours after 2 go (levels,
  variants).
* Against production: goals Z6 proves that production never did (if any); production-only goals by mechanism

## Interim (18:32, 3 hours)

* **Z6 41,789 at 3 hours against Z4's 37,962 (+3,827).** At 150 minutes: 40,541 against 37,900.
* **Z6 did not plateau.** In minutes 150-180 it gained 1,248, while Z4 gained 62 in that window. Its 5 s level
  proved 1,105 of 3,971 runs, after 287 of 3,036 in minutes 90-120: a cascade, new context reaching many goals at
  once. The kNN index is rebuilt every 10 minutes here, against 30 in Z4 (the one change from the defaults), and
  Z6 has the derived-rule transfer (its live loop: 635 proofs of 1,048 runs so far). Which of them started this
  burst is not measured; a later look at which lemmas the burst's proofs used can tell.
* The sweep (notes/2026-11-20-sweep20.md): 9 of 600 at 3 hours, 1.8 per CPU hour; chunk 1 proved 7 of 300.

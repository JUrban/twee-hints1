# Prover9: SFDF reorientation of proofs before they become hints

*Reconstructed on 17 Nov from REPORT 2n (13 Nov). Budgets in given clauses, so the runs are deterministic.*

**Question.** Veroff turns each proof into a strictly forward, demodulation-free (SFDF) proof before using its
steps as hints. Does that help Prover9 in our loop, and can it be done without search?

**Setup.**
* p9loop.py, Prover9-new master, on fam2 (aa1_15 and aK2_eq_aK3_q, 471 problems; the Prover9 control of
  2026-11-13-full-benchmark.md).
* Conversions:
  * `--convert expand`: `prooftrans expand` writes each rewrite step as its own step, so the proof is
    demodulation-free.
  * `--convert direct`: LADR's `directproof` gives a direct, forward, expanded proof.
  * The search-based conversion (rerun with the proof as hints under `set(restrict_denials)` and without
    demodulation) was tried on 34 proofs.
* Rounds of 150, 150, 150 and 220 givens. Hinted search either Prover9's default or SFDF settings.
* Scripts: exp/abs/p9*.sh.

**Results.** Both linear conversions take 9 ms per proof (279 proofs in 2.4 s). The search-based one
converted 2 of 34, at about 20 s each. Round 0 (150 givens, no hints) solves 237 in every version.

| hints | hinted search | round 1 | round 2 | round 3 | total |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| original proof steps | default | +26 | +9 | +11 | **283** |
| `prooftrans expand` (2.2× as many) | default | +26 | +9 | +7 | 279 |
| `directproof` (2.6× as many) | default | +26 | +9 | +7 | 279 |
| original proof steps | SFDF settings | +1 | +0 | +0 | 238 |
| `directproof` | SFDF settings | +3 | +0 | +0 | 240 |
| `directproof` | SFDF settings, 1,500 givens | +3 | | | 240 |

**Reading.**
* In the default search the extra steps never match. Round 1 is byte-identical in all three versions.
  Prover9 matches hints against kept clauses, and with demodulation on only normal forms are kept, so the
  intermediate equations the expansion adds never appear. Where the runs differ (round 3), the longer lists
  lose 4.
* The SFDF search can match them but is far weaker here (+3 even at 1,500 givens).
* What Veroff gets from SFDF proofs our hints already have: only positive steps, nothing from the denial,
  skolems generalised. For twee this holds by construction (2026-11-13-hyp-hints-abstract-time.md).
* Not the same as expanding lemma-based proofs back to the axioms (user, 17 Nov 18:57). That is a different
  expansion: it adds the derivations of the lemma axioms, which a proof's own steps lack
  (2026-11-17-expanded-proofs.md).

**Decision and next.** No SFDF conversion. REPORT 2n.

# Hub roots: the goals the from-zero runs lack, and what they hinge on (exp59)

Written 22 Nov 2026, 14:39 UTC.

## What production has that the from-zero runs do not

At exp/hubroots.py's run (14:36), Z7 (8 hours) and Z8 (6 hours; both from zero on all goals, 6 workers) together
have 43,994 goals, and 4,355 of production's are in neither. Their first own THM rows in loops/UL/r*/results.tsv: 3,526 are production's
own runs, and **2,082 of those fell at L0, the 5 s first-pass level** (L1 646, Vk100 268, Vk60 148, Vunf 145, Vk200 73,
L2 54). So most of the gap is not search effort: these goals fall in 5 s once the context has the right lemmas.

## The dependency graph (exp/hubroots.py, loops/HUB/roots.tsv)

* For each of the 4,355, the earliest kept twee output with a proof gives its first recorded proof, and its
  USEDAXIOMS line the lemma goals it used. 3807 have one.
* 502 are roots: their first proof used no other production-only goal as a lemma. The other 3305 used at least
  one.
* 184 roots have other production-only goals depending on them. A few carry most of them, through chains of kNN
  and sibling lemmas: aK2rev 349 (reach 1,980), Kproperties_1 314 (1,847), aKperms_1 342 (1,834), aa_instances_4 343
  (1,672), nil3_2K 365 (1,642), aKperms_1 318 (1,630), aK2_eq_aK3_1243b 446 and 440 (1,270, 1,267), aK2rev 376 (1,267).
  The reach counts dependence of first proofs, not necessity: a dependent goal might have had another proof.
* How production's own runs first proved the roots (first THM label): the top 10 L2 4, L1 3, L0 2, not its own runs 1;
  the top 24 L0 8, L1 7, L2 5, not its own runs 3, Vk100 1. The top ones' L1 and L2 runs (40 s and 80 s budgets) took 10 to 650
  CPU s, in production's fifth round. Some fell in earlier from-zero runs (Z6: Kproperties_1 314, nil3_29 377, aa_instances_4 282; ABd, UB, PGD).
* In Z7 and Z8 the top 10 roots had 2 to 8 runs each, mostly at L0, and most never had more than 8 CPU s.
* For 14 of the top 24 roots, every lemma axiom of the first proof is in Z8; the other 10 miss 1 or 2 (Z7's only).

So the from-zero runs may be failing on scheduling, not context: the hubs never got a long run.

## Measures and next

* Proved of 24, and CPU.
* If most prove: the hubs are a scheduling problem. Next, find a signal that picks them out from zero, for example
  how many unsolved goals would have a goal among their nearest kNN lemmas, and give those goals long runs early.
* If few prove: their proofs needed production's richer hints (pools), and the from-zero gap is context after all.

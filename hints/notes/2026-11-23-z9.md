# Z9: the system from zero on the whole benchmark, after exp55 and exp60

Written 22 Nov 2026, 23:37 UTC, before the launch (abs/after_exp60.sh starts it 10 minutes after exp60 ends, about 02:45).

## Question

What does the system as it stands prove from an empty context on all 51,784 goals, by 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8
hours? This measures the system; it is not an A/B. Runs at different times have differed by up to about 400 from the
machine's load, so Z9 against Z8 (22 Nov 02:53, 42,917 at 6 hours) or Z7 (21 Nov, 43,350 at 6 hours, 43,767 at 8)
is evidence only beyond that.

## Setup

* abs/z9.sh (exp/z9.sh): solve_new.sh's defaults, copied at the start to loops/Z9.solve_new.sh. Since Z8 they gained
  `--lemma-ax` in the derived-rule transfer (exp58a, 13:26) and `--knn-drift 5` in LATE (exp55, 20:35); exp60's
  P3B option (`--floor-ratio-until 120`) is not in (it trailed at 3 hours).
* sets/all, context loops/EMPTY, 6 workers, HOURS=8, SCHED= , XEVERY=300, LOOPARGS="--knn-refresh 600" (as Z8).
* twee: /project/bin/twee-pg61 (twee-e61 with PG 0.25 and the ground-joining branch limit 100); the transfer's
  twee-e39; rule model models/rm4t.
* CPUs 0-20,28-48 (taskset, inherited by the whole tree), apart from production's 21-27,49-55: in exp60 arms on a
  shared socket ran about 10% slower per abstract second (notes/2026-11-22-floor-ratio-until.md).
* Proofs: loop.py's proof jobs and exp/proofq.py print every proved run's proof into loops/Z9/proofs.

## Continuation past 8 hours: Z9C (planned 03:01)

**Why.** The decisions so far are read at 6 hours on half2, but production's extra goals came over days: the hub roots
need runs longer than a 6-hour run gives them, and their reach builds up later (exp59). Z8's last two hours spent
68-70% of their run CPU on goals production has solved and Z8 never did. How the from-zero system goes on after
8 hours, at 6 workers, is the long-horizon curve the goal asks about, and nothing else measures it.

**How.** abs/z9cont.sh (exp/z9cont.sh) starts 5 minutes after loops/Z9.done unless loops/Z9C.skip appears:
* the same loop.py command (saved from /proc at 02:47, loops/Z9.cmd.2737443) without `--init`, plus
  `--cascade-continue` (the restarted round replays r1/results.tsv: levels tried, yields, failures) and
  `--cascade-hours 16` (24 hours in all), on the same CPUs 0-20,28-48;
* the transfer and derived-rule side loops again with their saved commands (loops/Z9.cmd.2737562, .2737566), logs
  appended;
* loop.py rewrites config.json at its start, and setcurve.py and abscurve.py take its mtime as the start: it is set
  back to Z9's (kept in loops/Z9.t0), so the curve stays in minutes since Z9's start, the restart gap included;
* new rows' `t=` count from the restart again: loops/Z9.toffset ('ROW<tab>SECONDS') gives exp/abscurve.py and
  exp/latecpu.py the offset (both read it since 03:01).

**Smoke test** (abs/rst_smoke.sh, exp/ copy; sets/smoke487, 2 workers, 0.05 h, then the same command restarted with
`--cascade-continue --cascade-hours 0.05`, 23 Nov 02:53-02:59): 365 solved and 394 rows after the first part, 400
and 487 after the second; no traceback; none of the second part's 93 runs was on a goal solved in the first part,
and one repeated a (goal, level) pair of the first. With the offset file its abscurve times are monotone (last
365 s).

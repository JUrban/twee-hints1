# Why a from-zero half run stops short of production (20 Nov)

Written 20 Nov 2026, 13:47 UTC. An analysis of existing runs, no new runs. Script exp/halfgap.py.

## Question

The from-zero tests run on one half of the benchmark (sets/half1, sets/half2: 64 and 65 originals, none shared)
for 2 hours on 6 workers. At 2 hours they have about 20,000 of about 25,900 goals; production has about 24,000 of
each half. What do the goals production has and the arms lack need?

## Commands

    python3 exp/halfgap.py sets/half2 P2E,P2F     # exp41/exp42's arms
    python3 exp/halfgap.py sets/half1 PGE,PGD     # exp40's arms

## Results

| | half2 (P2E, P2F) | half1 (PGE, PGD) |
|---|---:|---:|
| goals | 25,847 | 25,937 |
| production | 24,222 | 23,950 |
| the arms (union) | 20,454 | 19,961 |
| production only | 3,768 | 3,989 |
| ... first proved by production's 5 s level (L0) | 2,155 | 2,167 |
| ... L1 (the 20 s chain level) / L2 | 498 / 58 | 639 / 65 |
| ... 5 s variants (V*) | 383 | 401 |
| ... side writers (transfer, live loop, re-proofs, joins) | about 260 | about 250 |
| median goal number, production only / the arms | 403 / 170 | 423 / 172 |
| **L0 proofs with a record that used a lemma from outside the half** | **1,363 of 1,714** | **1,281 of 1,688** |
| ... used a lemma of the half the first arm had not proved (and none outside) | 340 | 393 |
| ... all lemmas proved by the first arm (or none used) | 11 | 14 |

* The goals production has and the arms lack are deep (median goal number 403 against 170). Most fell to
  production's cheapest level, 5 s, so they were not hard runs once their context existed.
* **The missing context is mostly the other half.** 80% (half2) and 76% (half1) of those 5 s proofs used a kNN
  lemma from an original of the other half. A half run can never have such a lemma.
* Production picked those lemmas because it had them. Without them kNN picks others, and some of these proofs
  would go through anyway. So this is an upper bound on what the split costs, not a measure of it.
* Only 11 and 14 of the proofs used lemmas all of which the arm had: the arms do not fail for want of trying
  with the right context.

## Consequences

* **The half tests understate the system.** The cross-original kNN lemmas were the main source of production's
  gains (19 Nov assessment), and the split cuts them in half. A change that helps through cross-original lemmas
  shows less on a half.
* The half tests stay right for A/B decisions (same handicap on both arms, two arms at once).
* **The long from-zero run belongs on the whole benchmark**, as Z0-Z4 were (Z4, the 17 Nov system: 37,713 at 2
  hours, 38,044 at about 4 hours, 12 workers). Today's two half arms at 2 hours sum to 39,743 (PGE 19,591 + P2E
  20,152) on 2 × 6 workers, without cross-half lemmas. Z6 (planned after exp43): today's defaults on all 51,784, 12
  workers, 8 hours, against Z4's curve.

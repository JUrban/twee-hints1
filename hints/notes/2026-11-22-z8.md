# Z8: the system as it stands, from zero on all goals (22 Nov, planned after exp53)

**Question.** exp52 added `--floor-ratio 20` to solve_new.sh's LATE (21 Nov 23:51) after +789 at 3 hours on half2
with 4 workers. Its lead narrowed by 251 in the third hour. On the whole benchmark over 8 hours, does it keep the
early gain without a late cost?

## Setup

* abs/z8.sh (exp/z8.sh): one arm, Z8, solve_new.sh's defaults at the start (LATE with --floor-ratio 20, and anything
  exp53 adds), as Z7 (abs/z7pair.sh): an empty context (loops/EMPTY), sets/all (51,784 goals), 6 workers, 8 hours,
  SCHED=, transfer every 5 minutes, `--knn-refresh 600`.
* Started by abs/after_exp53.sh (exp/after_exp53.sh, PID 1485120) when exp53 ends (about 02:52): it waits up to 20
  minutes for loops/Z8.go or loops/Z8.skip, so that exp53's decision can be installed first.
* Binaries: /project/bin/twee-pg61 (twee-e61 with TWEE_PG_ARGS), loop.py and solve_new.sh at the commit current at
  the start.

## Why one arm

A pair (Z8 against a Z7 replica) needs 12 workers and production's CPU, as the Z7 pair did. Z7's curve is the
reference instead. Cross-time differences under about 400 are not evidence (exp/abscurve.py leaves 167-426 of a
replica's gap), but the early effect should be larger: Z7 trailed its control by 2,368 at 1 hour, and that cost is
what the floors that wait remove.

## Measures

* abs/setcurve.py sets/all Z8 Z7 Z7C Z6 --at 30,60,120,180,240,360,480; exp/abscurve.py for equal abstract work.
* exp/quickstat.py Z8 --bins 60,120,240,480: runs, proofs and CPU by kind and time.
* The mix lines: when the q and h floors come back.
* Decision: none to make; a reading. If Z8 trails Z7 by more than about 400 after 4 hours, the floor ratio goes into
  a side-by-side test at 8 hours before it stays in production's defaults.

# First hints in twee: baselines, self-replay, and the first hint loops on dev10

*Reconstructed on 17 Nov from REPORT 2, the commits 641a53b–1b4626c, and the session's commands. All runs
before 13 Nov 09:30 used CPU-time limits, not abstract time, so their counts carry load noise; repeated
identical runs differed by about 20 on 3,052 problems (1,753 against 1,771).*

**Question.** The goal (user, 12 Nov 19:30): Veroff-style hint guidance for twee on AIM60k, iterating on small
subsets. First: does equation-hint matching work in twee at all, and does a hint loop gain over plain twee?

**Setup.**
* **twee changes** (twee-h1 … twee-h9, 12 Nov 19:40–20:43):
  * `--hints-file` (lhs, rhs, optional factor).
  * Matching on canonical variants in either orientation; a matching critical pair's score is multiplied by
    0.01.
  * Hints are re-normalised by the oriented rules (Prover9's back_demod_hints).
  * Goal-constant expansion before matching: twee flattens goals into constants, and hints are stored with
    those expanded and skolems as variables.
  * Consider-emulation: also match pairs that ordered rewriting would turn into a hint.
  * Fingerprint prefilter (a hash of the symbol multiset).
  * `--eqhint-requeue F`: re-simplify the queue when new rules matched hints.
* **dev10** (sets/dev10, made 19:34): all 3,052 goals of 10 originals:
  aK1_to_aK2 mbol_com aa1_15 aK2_eq_aK3_q a_of_aa aa2_to_aa3 nil3_15 aa1_45_c Ka_eq_aK3 aK2_eq_aK3_b.
  REPORT calls them "of mixed difficulty"; how I picked them is not recorded.
* **Baselines:** hints/run.py at 2 s, 20 workers, plain twee and `--no-flatten-goal` (runs/base_t2,
  runs/base_nf_t2). The E and Prover9 numbers are the AIM60k README's, at 1 s.
* **Self-replay:** aK2_eq_aK3_b__goal_315 rerun with the 89 generalised lemmas of its own twee proof, adding
  one feature at a time.
* **Loops:**
  * L1: hints/loop.py with pool = all proofs of the same original (`loop.py sets/dev10 loops/L1 --rounds 6
    --timeout 2 -j 20 --twee twee-h4`).
  * L2: starts from L1's round 0 with a kNN pool (`--init loops/L1 --pool knn:30:100`, twee-h7).
* **Hints against extra time:** plain twee 8 s on what round 0 left (`run.py sets/dev10_unsolved_r0
  runs/base_t8`, twee-h8, -j 10), against L2 continued at 8 s for 2 rounds (twee-h8r, -j 10).

| setting | rules | time |
|---|---:|---:|

**Reading.**
* Goal flattening must stay on (1,753 against 1,176), so hints must match through the flattening constants.
  Without expansion they barely help even the problem's own proof.
* Requeue matters because twee keeps queued pairs as they were made. A pair made before the rule that turns
  it into a hint never matches. diag2.py showed exactly those late lemmas.
* Hint matching cost about 30% of throughput before the fingerprint prefilter, about 7% after (same search,
  3,641 → 2,558 rules in 5 s before).
* The fair comparison at the same final limit: hints add only +17 (1,945 against 1,928). Most dev10 goals fall
  early or not at all, so at 2–8 s there is little room for hints. This is the user's later point (20:51):
  hints matter in long runs.
* The self-replay is a best case: the hints are the problem's own proof.

**Decision and next.** Keep goal-constant expansion and the prefilter. Requeue helps the self-replay but not
transfer from other proofs (2026-11-12-hint-selection.md). Hint selection next
(2026-11-12-hint-selection.md); the rule model (2026-11-12-rule-model.md). REPORT 1, 2.

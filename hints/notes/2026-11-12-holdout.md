# The AIM60k holdout: plain twee, the rule model, and the hint loop

*Reconstructed on 17 Nov from REPORT 2e and the session's commands. CPU-time limits; 20 processes on the
112-core machine.*

**Question.** On the README's reporting split (holdout.names, 5,880 problems), with nothing trained on it, how
do the rule model and the hint loop compare with plain twee at the same final limit?

**Setup.**
* H0, plain twee (twee-e6, from 12 Nov 21:38, tmp/base_holdout.sh): `run.py sets/holdout runs/H0_t2
  --timeout 2 -j 6`, then 8 s on what it left (runs/H0_t8).
* H3: twee + rule model rm3 at 2 s (stopped after the 2 s round).
* H2, the hint loop (from 22:16):

      loop.py sets/holdout loops/H2 --rounds 4 --timeout 2,2,8,8 -j 10 --twee twee-e9 --pool knn:30:100
        --model --model-fixed models/rm3.bin,models/rm3.vocab
        --model-args "--rule-threshold 0.1 --rule-penalty 2" --hints-extra hints/train_pool --hints-from-start

  * Its pool starts as the proofs of the training problems (hints/train_pool, 8,562, holdout problems
    skipped). The loop then adds its own holdout proofs.
* rm3: given/train_only, i.e. tr30 + dev10's round 0 without any holdout problem
  (2026-11-12-rule-model.md).
* Threshold and penalty were tuned on the 818 goals of 3 dev10 originals.

  a fixed limit.

**Reading.**
* **What holdout is** (AIM60k README, "Train, devel, holdout and secret"): 11 whole originals that are unseen
  (4,720 problems), plus 1,160 late goals moved out of the 115 devel and train originals. Checked 17 Nov:
  holdout has 126 originals, and 11 of them have no train or devel problem.
* **Leaks found on 17 Nov:**
  * dev10, on which the first design choices were made (kNN 30:100, requeue, the model's settings), contains
    two whole holdout originals: aK1_to_aK2 (80 holdout problems) and a_of_aa (300). It also has 66 moved
    late goals. rm1 was trained on dev10's round 0 including them.
  * The tuning set for threshold and penalty (818 goals of aa1_45_c, mbol_com, nil3_15) had 22 moved late
    goals.
  * Clean: rm3's training data and the hint pool skipped every holdout problem (given/train_only,
    hints/train_pool).
  * So REPORT 2e's "nothing below is trained or tuned on it" holds for the data, not quite for the design.
    This leak is in the choice of methods and two numbers, not in what the runs learn.
* For the 1,160 moved goals, sibling training goals of the same proof are in the pool and the model's data,
  by the split's design.
* Most problems lost at 2 s are boundary cases that plain twee solves in 1–2 s.

**Decision and next.** The rule model and kNN hints are on in every later loop. The whole public benchmark
next (2026-11-13-full-benchmark.md). REPORT 2e.
